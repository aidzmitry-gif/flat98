The picture unwraps a continuous clockwise turn around this single camera; the toilet door at the far right is behind the observer, NOT next to the kitchen in actual space. Render a true continuous interior panorama with gentle cylindrical curvature of the floor and ceiling. No split panels, diagram, room text, colored blocks, labels, numbers, people or pets.

Reference 1 is the binding 360-degree geometric guide of THIS apartment. Its gray surfaces are walls, brown is the end of the oak cabinet, and the marked colored rectangles are OPENINGS, not colored doors. Preserve their exact left-to-right ordering and the relative approximate positions. The tiny green code badges are guide annotations only: do not render text. Reference 2 is the real, unfinished entrance photograph; use it only as secondary evidence of the real black entrance and kitchen opening. Do not reproduce the people. Reference 3 is the last kitchen-side visual of the current natural-oak, mirrored cabinet and small olive bench: copy its finishes and design only, not its camera.

From left to right around the panorama: D5 is a narrow oblique opening on the left wall into the dressing room; P6 is the opening at the end of the short left arm to the bedroom. Next is the exposed near end of the SAME 1050-mm-wide, 580-mm-deep light oak hallway cabinet, standing on the entrance's existing left wall. It is furniture, not a new room partition. The neighboring 575-mm bench is closer to the entrance but almost hidden from this camera. Near the middle is exactly one matte-black entrance door D1, aligned with the reference. D2 to its right is the open kitchen side passage in the kitchen partition, not a second entrance door. P2 is the opening at the far end of the right arm to the children's room. D3 is the bathroom opening on the right arm's near side wall. The last and nearest opening D4 is the toilet doorway behind the observer, visible only because this is a 360-degree panorama. Use conventional pale doors or open doorframes at D3/D4/D5, with their leaves open enough to understand the passages; do not suggest specific hinges. Keep P6 and P2 as passages, without inventing additional fixed doors or moving walls.

Replace the guide colors with believable furnished-room glimpses or neutral finished interiors; do not blur the side wings. Restrained warm off-white plaster walls, light natural oak floor, matching oak wardrobe, soft warm recessed ceiling lighting, realistic proportions and photographic materials, moderate dynamic range. Preserve the actual compact apartment feeling: the long 360-degree strip represents different directions, not a long straight hallway. Do not add a wall across the connection between entry and corridor; do not create a duplicate passage, an extra door, a second kitchen, a laundry appliance, a robot vacuum, room labels or dimension annotations.
